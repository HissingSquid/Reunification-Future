Interstellar Heavy Starliners are the largest classification of [[starliner]], capable of carrying many thousands of people across vast distances, usually very quickly. Due to their vast size they are rarely atmospheric-capable and therefore require extensive [[Orbital Space Port|orbital infrastructure]], as well as support from [[auto-lighters]] or [[landing ferries]]. Additionally, their significant upfront costs mean they are typically operated only on the very busiest routes, where their unparalleled transport capacity makes them more cost-effective over the long-term compared to their smaller cousins.
