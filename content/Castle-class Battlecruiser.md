The Castle-class was a class of two [[Battlecruiser|battlecruisers]] built for the [[Reunification Navy]] between 3371 and 3375. They were the second class of battlecruisers to be acquired, with design and construction work starting in earnest after the [[Sixth Kharak-Ghar War]], having been suspended during the conflict in favour of a focus on more established capabilities and more pressing needs. 

In the end only two ships were produced due to post-war cutbacks and the already bloated nature of the fleet following wartime acquisitions. This marked a temporary end to the [[Battlecruiser Programme]], though the project would be revived in 3394 with the development of the [[Constellation-class]].
