JJ Adenyi is a freelance mercenary based in [[Neo-Atlantica]], primarily known for his leadership and organisational abilities. A hulking figure and a formidable fighter, Adenyi is regularly called upon to run some of the toughest gigs in the city, capable of getting even the most disparate teams running like a well-oiled machine. 

Adenyi is a former soldier who served with [[Alliance Combat Forces]] during the suppression of the [[The Jeruvian Rebellion|Jeruvian Rebellion]]. He was discharged from service in 3349 after reports emerged of brutality and violent conduct, including the alleged rape of several civilians. However, he managed to slip free from custody while in transit and escaped in a light shuttle, leaving behind the corpses of his three guards. After hitch-hiking halfway across neutral space, he arrived on [[High Atlantic]] in 3354, and took to his new career as a freebooter like a duck to water. 

**Abilities**
Adenyi is a natural leader, an excellent motivator, and a highly-trained Alliance soldier. He would likely have enjoyed a successful ACF career were it not for his cruel streak and poor personal impulse control. He is also a formidable physical specimen, further augmented by the [[Alliance Basic Enhancements package]], and he was fitted with a [[Neural Link]], of which he is an above-average user. 

His signature weapons are an Alliance-issue [[C-27 Coilgun]], and a custom-made sawn-off [[Shardgun]].

**Appearance**
Adenyi is a tall, powerful man, some 6'8" in height and bulging with muscle. He keeps his head shaved bald, but does cultivate a well-groomed beard that is his pride and joy. A pragmatic man, he is usually found in his personal suit of heavily-graffitied [[Power Armour]], though occasionally he will just opt for a heavy plate carrier instead if circumstances would render the full rig impractical.
