Nessa Bouchien is a contract assassin, primarily working for [[Hacienda]] in the city of [[Neo-Atlantica]]. A former [[Companions|Companion]], she is a close personal friend of [[Sierra Navarro]], with the two of them having worked together on numerous jobs for [[Mr Michigan]]. Bouchien also has significant ties with the [[Safariland Tribal Union]], having grown up on one of [[High Atlantic]]'s nature reserves before being scouted by the Companions. 

**Abilities**
Bouchien is considered one of the most capable snipers on High Atlantic, most often hired for overwatch or assassination contracts. This is an unusual skillset for a Companion, originally honed during her time as a young poacher on the reserves and later utilised by the Guild for a series of special operations - operations that ultimately gave her the freedom to escape from their clutches. 

In addition to her outstanding marksmanship, Bouchien is also trained in the usual hand-to-hand combat and social manipulation skills of a Companion, though she is unusually lightly modified - it was felt bionics would impede her natural affinity for stealth and concealment. 

Uncommonly for a sniper, Bouchien does not favour any particular weapon, adapting her arsenal to suit the specific circumstances of a job. She is known to have a particular love for [[Accuracy Interstellar]] weapons, however, for their handmade quality. 

**Personality**
Bouchien is unusually anxious for a contract killer, perhaps explaining her preference for keeping her targets at arms length. Still, she does have the steel of a woman who has taken a life, and her somewhat vulnerable and unassuming demeanour belies a ruthless trigger finger.
