This database is designed to catalogue all of the important places, people, events, and technology of the Reunification Future Universe I have created. It is intended to have near-endless scope for expansion and ongoing development. 

There is no 'present day' set for each individual article, though they are generally written in the present tense where a character is still alive and occasionally pick a time earlier than the current place in the narrative (often around 3370). Where a character is significantly involved in major plot events, their present tense may be set at an early point in their involvement, with a *Story* section that summarises their narrative role from that point on and should be avoided if you do not want the plot spoiled.
