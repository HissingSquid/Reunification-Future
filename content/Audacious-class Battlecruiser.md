The Audacious-class was a class of three [[Battlecruiser|battlecruisers]] built for the [[Reunification Navy]] between 3352 and 3361 as part of the [[Battlecruiser Programme]], a component of the wider [[Fleet Modernisation Initiative]]. 

While the Battlecruiser Programme was suspended for the duration of the [[Sixth Kharak-Ghar War]], the Audacious-class proved to be a successful design, thanks primarily to its impressive jump range, and consequently excellent strategic mobility. This meant that despite their tactical inflexibility, the ships were a very mobile source of heavy naval firepower that could respond quickly to emerging threats.
