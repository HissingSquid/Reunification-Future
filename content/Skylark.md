*Skylark* is a [[Raven-class ECMCW Frigate]] in service with the [[Reunification Navy]]. She was commissioned in 3354 as the second ship of the class, serving as a proof-of-concept for further vessels. 

She is best known for being the ship on which [[Loeh Kruval]] served in the early years of the [[Sixth Kharak-Ghar War]].
