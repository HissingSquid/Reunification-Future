The Terran Reunification Alliance (TRA) was one of the founding nations of the [[Reunification Alliance]], and perhaps the closest thing to its direct predecessor. Prior to its integration into the Alliance, the TRA comprised the government of [[Terra]] and the [[Sol System Management Authority]] (SSMA), which was responsible for managing [[Human|humanity]]'s colonies and spacecraft. The SSMA would later become the [[Alliance Astrotime Authority]] following the integration of [[Centauran|centauran]] members.
