Mr Michigan is the mysterious and enigmatic figure at the heart of the [[High Atlantic Resistance Movement]], known for his distinctive white suit and ruthless methods. Thought to be a founding member of [[Hacienda]], one of the most prestigious Street Music Organisations, he is closely associated with the gang's direct-action arm and widely considered to be the driving force behind its increasingly close ties with the [[Safariland Tribal Union]] - and the resulting proliferation of fusion bands on the Hacienda label. 

Exact details of his life, identity, and whereabouts are unknown, even to the most influential [[Spiders]]. There are even rumours that 'Mr Michigan' is a title rather than an individual, afforded to major players in the concealed upper echelons of the Resistance Movement. What is known is that he controls almost every aspect of direct-action against the [[Tourist Board]], personally managing the myriad of planet-wide operations intended to undermine their influence and pave the way for the [[Reunification Alliance]] to return.
