*Nightingale* is a [[Raven-class ECMCW Frigate|Raven-class]] [[Electronic Warfare Frigate]] of the [[Reunification Navy]], commissioned in 3361 as the first of the Flight II production run. She is mostly widely known for her role in [[Operation Fulcrum]] during the [[Sixth Kharak-Ghar War]], in which she served as the Alliance's primary SIGINT unit monitoring the build-up of [[Kharak-Ghar Republic of Militant Citizens|Kharak-Ghar]] naval units ahead of the [[Arven Deep-Space Action]].
