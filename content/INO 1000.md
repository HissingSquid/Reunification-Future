The INO 1000 is a list of the thousand richest private companies operating under the purview of the [[League of Interstellar Civilisations]]. It is maintained by the [[Interstellar News Organisation]], the public relations arm of the LIC. Numerous analysts take issues with the INO 1000, particularly since its qualifying requirement exclude [[Independent Enterprises]] and [[Autonomous Design Bureaus]] - the closest things the [[Reunification Alliance]] has to private corporations. 

Somewhat surprisingly, the INO 1000 is dominated by companies operating in the [[Non-aligned Worlds]], alongside, less surprisingly, those of the [[Celestian Empire]].
